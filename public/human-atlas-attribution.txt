Upstream adaptations: source OBJ geometry converted to meters/Y-up, translated,
simplified with a 0.2% relative error limit, normals quantized, packed into binary chunks.

Anatomy Atelier adaptations (2026-09-07): selected 227 source meshes into 25
anatomical topics; retained source identities and relative positions; assigned
materials; converted to GLB with Meshopt compression; generated Chinese labels
and surface landmarks; rendered 720px and 180px WebP navigation images from these
models. The model-derived images are also distributed under CC BY 4.0.
